## Shopping Tips

### ✅ Do:
- Check reviews and ratings (4+ stars)
- Look for "Amazon's Choice" or "Best Seller" badges
- Verify delivery date (before Diwali for current deals)
- Compare prices from different sellers
- Order 2 sensors if budget allows (backup)

### ❌ Avoid:
- Items with no reviews
- Sellers with <90% positive ratings
- "Too good to be true" prices
- Unclear specifications
